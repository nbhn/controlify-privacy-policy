Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 27, 2024

Controlify ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information specifically regarding our IoT temperature monitoring services.

1. Information We Collect

Controlify is a privacy-first application. We collect only the minimal information required for the app to function:

  • SMS Messages: Used exclusively for IoT device control. The app only processes messages from the hardware devices you have explicitly registered.
  • Device Configuration: Local settings for your temperature monitor zones are stored on your device.

What we do NOT collect:

  • We do NOT collect personal identification (names, emails, etc.).
  • We do NOT use usage analytics or tracking services.
  • We do NOT transmit any of your data to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Control IoT devices through SMS commands.
  • Monitor and display real-time temperature and humidity data.
  • Ensure app security and prevent unauthorized device access.
  • Provide local status updates and historical data tracking.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Processing: All SMS processing and parsing are done entirely on your local device.
  • No Cloud Storage: Your temperature data and device settings never leave your phone.
  • Encryption: Local data storage is encrypted where applicable to prevent unauthorized access by other apps.

4. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access & Export: You can view all processed data within the app's dashboard.
  • Deletion: You can delete your local configuration and data history at any time through the app settings.
  • Consent: You can withdraw SMS permissions through your Android system settings at any time.
